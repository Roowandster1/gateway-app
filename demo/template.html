Why this is not a chatbot

  • Packs70g of lentils costs a whole 99p bag. Cost is counted over whole packs bought, which is what forces reuse.
  • LeftoversRice and oil carry over. Chicken and bread rot. The solver is penalised only for the second.
  • No fitIt returns infeasible and names the binding constraint. That is an answer.
  • PricesSeed estimates with a source and a date, flagged unverified. Nothing is guessed.
  • The modelWrites cooking steps and nothing else. A checker rejects any step stating an amount.
